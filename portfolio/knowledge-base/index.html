Quick Answer

The current fictional WC-100 requirements covered by this article are:

Maximum allowable wear: 12 mm

Minimum mounting clearance: 15 mm

The article directs users to the applicable controlled technical documentation for authoritative and detailed requirements.

Knowledge Design Approach

The article follows a user-first knowledge design approach.

  • The question is clearly stated.
  • The answer appears near the beginning.
  • Important values are easy to locate.
  • Short sections improve scanning.
  • Common questions are addressed.
  • Users receive clear next-step guidance.
  • Escalation instructions are provided for exceptions.

Knowledge Management Approach

The article demonstrates how a technical writer can transform detailed engineering information into a concise knowledge resource for users who need quick answers.

The content is structured for searchability, findability, scannability, accuracy, actionability and maintainability.

Documentation Governance

The article is treated as a managed knowledge asset rather than static content.

The lifecycle includes creation, technical review, SME validation, approval, publication, monitoring, review, update and retirement when the content is no longer required.

Enterprise Documentation Environment

The knowledge article is designed for use within a structured enterprise documentation and knowledge-management environment.

Relevant enterprise platforms may include Teamcenter, Windchill, SharePoint, ServiceNow and other approved knowledge or document-management systems, depending on organisational configuration.
