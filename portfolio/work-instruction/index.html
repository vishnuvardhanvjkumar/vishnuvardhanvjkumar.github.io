WC-100 Industrial Wear Component

Manufacturing Work Instruction — Final Inspection and Deburring

Project Overview

This demonstration project presents a manufacturing work instruction for the final dimensional inspection and deburring of a fictional industrial wear component.

The document demonstrates how technical information can be converted into a clear, sequential and operator-focused instruction while maintaining quality, safety, traceability and document-control requirements.

  • Traceability and record control
  • Document revision and lifecycle management

Documentation Approach

The work instruction follows a task-oriented structure that allows a manufacturing user to understand what must be done, in what order, what tools are required, what must be checked and what action is required when a result does not meet specification.

The instruction is intentionally linked to the WC-100 Product Specification developed as Portfolio 01, demonstrating how multiple controlled documents can support the same product lifecycle.
